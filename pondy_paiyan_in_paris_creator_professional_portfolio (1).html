Tamil warmth meet Parisian savoir-faire

Growing up in Pondicherry (Puducherry), the lingering French colonial heritage—yellow stucco villas, boulevard signboards, and French language schools—was part of daily life. Yet, moving to France to pursue a Master’s degree at Montpellier Business School (MBS) transformed that distant cultural kinship into a tangible reality.

Between mastering French administrative labyrinths, discovering the best budget boulangeries, and breaking through professional corporate sectors in Europe, Pondy Paiyan in Paris was founded. It serves as an unvarnished, humorous, and heartfelt digital haven for dreamers, students, and culture enthusiasts across India and the global Tamil diaspora.
